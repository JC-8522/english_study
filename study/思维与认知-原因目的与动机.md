1. **conscience**，读音：/ˈkɑn.ʃəns/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：良心；口语：① [You shouldn't need to compromise with your conscience.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/4311075) ② [I'm calm because I have a clear conscience.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/11772912)

2. **impulse**，读音：/ˈɪm.pəls/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：冲动；驱使；口语：① [I barely restrained the impulse to strike him.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/260908) ② [I felt an impulse to cry out loud.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/275778)

3. **incentives**，读音：/ˌɪn.ˈsɛn.tɪvz/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：激励某人做某事的事物( incentive的复数形式 )；刺激；口语：① [They convinced Tom to stay by offering gargantuan incentives.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/11746079) ② [He was incensed by their lack of incentives.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/302550)

4. **mania**，读音：/ˈmeɪ.ni.ə/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：狂躁；热衷；口语：① [I don't understand all the soccer mania.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/552149) ② [I get hypomanic, but rarely does this become full-blown mania.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/13580529)

5. **obsession**，读音：/əb.ˈsɛ.ʃən/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：困扰；困扰人的情绪；口语：① [I can't understand his obsession with football.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/2568321) ② Can we talk about the obsession for a minute?

6. **urge**，读音：/ɝdʒ/，重音：单音节，中文：冲动；推动力；力劝 / I urge you to reconsider.(我强烈劝你重新考虑。)；口语：① [I couldn't resist the urge to applaud.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/1172529) ② [I can't resist the urge to eat raw meat.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/2758112)

7. **urges**，读音：/ˈɝ.dʒəz/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：强烈的愿望( urge的名词复数 )；强烈的欲望；口语：① [You awaken primal urges in me.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/10005693) ② [Sami's urges to smoke weed were coming back.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/7241989)
