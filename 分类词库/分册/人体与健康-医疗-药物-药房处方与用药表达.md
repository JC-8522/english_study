# 人体与健康 / 医疗 / 药物 / 药房处方与用药表达

[返回分类目录](../README.md)

1. **doses**，读音：/ˈdoʊ.səz/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：服药（dose的三单形式）；口语：① [We were only given infinitesimal doses.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/10522563) ② [Both vaccines require two doses for full potency.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/9700753)

2. **drug interaction**，读音：/drʌɡ ˌɪn.tɚ.ˈæk.ʃən/，重音：可重读 interaction（主重音第3音节），中文：两种药物之间可能相互影响的情况；口语：① I asked the pharmacist about a possible drug interaction. ② Does the label mention any drug interactions?

3. **generic equivalent**，读音：/dʒə.ˈnɛ.rɪk ɪ.ˈkwɪ.və.lənt/，重音：可重读 generic（主重音第2音节），中文：与品牌药相对应的仿制药；口语：① Is there a generic equivalent for this? ② The pharmacist said they had a generic equivalent in stock.

4. **get a refill**，读音：/ɡɛt ə ˈri.ˌfɪl/，重音：可重读 refill（主重音第1音节），中文：把处方药再配一份；口语：① I need to get a refill before we leave. ② Can I get a refill at this pharmacy?

5. **injection**，读音：/ˌɪn.ˈdʒɛk.ʃən/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：注射；注射剂；口语：① [I'm going to give you an injection.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/11586374) ② [I'll give you a painkilling injection.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/278321)

6. **medications**，读音：/ˌmɛ.də.ˈkeɪ.ʃənz/，重音：主重音第3音节，中文：药物治疗；药物（medication的复数形式）；口语：① I asked the pharmacist about medications. ② Did you tell your doctor about the medications?

7. **medicinal**，读音：/mə.ˈdɪ.sə.nəl/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：药用的；药的；口语：① [We have a large variety of aromatic and medicinal plants.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/13772693) ② [Mary gathered some medicinal plants in the forest.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/6620356)

8. **medicines**，读音：/ˈmɛ.də.sənz/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：药( medicine的复数形式 )；内服药；口语：① I asked the pharmacist about medicines. ② Did you tell your doctor about the medicines?

9. **miss a dose**，读音：/mɪs ə ˈdoʊs/，重音：可重读 dose，中文：漏服一次应服的药；口语：① I think I missed a dose yesterday. ② What should I do if I miss a dose?

10. **over-the-counter medicine**，读音：/ˈoʊ.vɚ ðə ˈkaʊn.tɚ ˈmɛ.də.sən/，重音：可重读 counter（主重音第1音节），中文：无需处方就能购买的药；口语：① Does this count as an over-the-counter medicine? ② I asked the pharmacist about an over-the-counter medicine.

11. **pharmaceutical**，读音：/ˌfɑr.mə.ˈsu.tɪ.kəl/，重音：主重音第3音节，中文：药学的；制药的；口语：① I asked the pharmacist about pharmaceutical. ② Did you tell your doctor about the pharmaceutical?

12. **pharmaceuticals**，读音：/ˌfɑr.mə.ˈsu.tɪ.kəlz/，重音：主重音第3音节，中文：医药品；口语：① I asked the pharmacist about pharmaceuticals. ② Did you tell your doctor about the pharmaceuticals?

13. **pharmacies**，读音：/ˈfɑr.mə.siz/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：制药( pharmacy的复数形式 )；药店；口语：① I asked the pharmacist about pharmacies. ② Did you tell your doctor about the pharmacies?

14. **pick up a prescription**，读音：/pɪk ʌp ə prə.ˈskrɪp.ʃən/，重音：可重读 prescription（主重音第2音节），中文：去药房领取处方药；口语：① I need to pick up a prescription after work. ② Can I pick up my prescription before six?

15. **pill**，读音：/pɪl/，重音：单音节，中文：药丸；弹丸；口语：① [I'm giving a pill to the cat.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/372432) ② [I'd like to take the pill.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/34455)

16. **prescriptions**，读音：/prə.ˈskrɪp.ʃənz/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：医药处方；药方（prescription复数形式）；口语：① [Antonio found the fake prescriptions in Rima's pockets.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/11775848) ② [I need my prescriptions.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/5658186)

17. **refill**，读音：/ˈri.fɪl/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：再注满；替换物；口语：① I asked the pharmacist about refill. ② Did you tell your doctor about the refill?

18. **refills**，读音：/ri.ˈfɪlz/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：（放在容器中）可重新注满某物的材料( refill的名词复数 )；口语：① I asked the pharmacist about refills. ② Did you tell your doctor about the refills?

19. **tablets**，读音：/ˈtæ.bləts/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：药片( tablet的名词复数 )；（木、竹）简；口语：① [I can't swallow these tablets without a drink of water.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/270737) ② [Do you think it's wise to wash your tablets down with beer?](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/12536168)

20. **take with food**，读音：/teɪk wɪð ˈfud/，重音：可重读 food，中文：和食物同服；口语：① The label says to take it with food. ② Do I need to take this with food?

21. **wear off**，读音：/wɛr ˈɔf/，重音：可重读 off，中文：药效、感觉或影响逐渐消退；口语：① When will the numbness wear off? ② I could feel the medicine starting to wear off.
