# 家居与日用 / 住宅 / 房屋设施与装修

1. **caulk around**，读音：/ˈkɑk ɚ.ˈaʊnd/，重音：可重读 caulk，中文：在接缝周围打密封胶；口语：① We need to caulk around the tub. ② Did you caulk around the window yet?

2. **circuit breaker**，读音：/ˈsɝ.kət ˈbreɪ.kɚ/，重音：可重读 circuit（主重音第1音节），中文：过载或故障时断电的断路器；口语：① I think we tripped a circuit breaker. ② Which circuit breaker controls the garage?

3. **get a quote**，读音：/ɡɛt ə ˈkwoʊt/，重音：可重读 quote，中文：找商家或施工方报个价；口语：① I'm getting a quote for the bathroom floor. ② Did you get a quote from the other contractor?

4. **hardwood floor**，读音：/ˈhɑr.ˌdwʊd flɔr/，重音：hardwood：主重音第1音节；floor：单音节，中文：木地板；口语：① I'd love a place with a hardwood floor. ② Be careful not to scratch the hardwood floor.

5. **load-bearing wall**，读音：/loʊd ˈbɛ.rɪŋ wɔl/，重音：可重读 bearing（主重音第1音节），中文：支撑建筑重量的承重墙；口语：① Do you know if that's a load-bearing wall? ② The contractor said we need to check whether it's a load-bearing wall.

6. **patch a hole**，读音：/pætʃ ə ˈhoʊl/，重音：可重读 hole，中文：修补一个破洞；口语：① I patched a hole in the drywall this morning. ② Can you help me patch the hole behind the door?

7. **shutoff valve**，读音：/ˈʃʌt.ˌɔf ˈvælv/，重音：可重读 valve，中文：用来切断水流或气流的阀门；口语：① Do you know where the shutoff valve is? ② The plumber replaced the shutoff valve under the sink.

8. **smart home**，读音：/smɑrt hoʊm/，重音：smart：单音节；home：单音节，中文：智能家居；口语：① Do you really need all those smart home devices? ② We're slowly turning our place into a smart home.

9. **touch-up paint**，读音：/tʌtʃ ʌp ˈpeɪnt/，重音：可重读 paint，中文：补涂小面积损伤用的漆；口语：① Do we have any touch-up paint for this wall? ② I'll grab some touch-up paint for the doorframe.

10. **uninhabitable**，读音：/ˌʌ.nɪn.ˈhæ.bə.tə.bəl/，重音：主重音第3音节，中文：不适合居住的；口语：① [Can Earth become uninhabitable?](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/11802393) ② [Large portions of the Algerian desert are uninhabitable.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/7860065)

11. **up to code**，读音：/ʌp tə ˈkoʊd/，重音：可重读 code，中文：符合适用的建筑或安全规范；口语：① The inspector said the wiring is up to code. ② We need to bring the stairs up to code.

12. **wall paint**，读音：/wɔl peɪnt/，重音：wall：单音节；paint：单音节，中文：墙漆；口语：① What color wall paint did you choose? ② We still have some wall paint left over.

13. **water damage**，读音：/ˈwɔ.tɚ ˈdæ.mədʒ/，重音：可重读 water（主重音第1音节），中文：渗水、漏水等造成的损坏；口语：① There's water damage under the kitchen sink. ② Did you notice any water damage by the window?

14. **weather stripping**，读音：/ˈwɛ.ðɚ ˈstrɪ.pɪŋ/，重音：可重读 weather（主重音第1音节），中文：门窗边用于挡风、防漏的密封条；口语：① The weather stripping is peeling off the back door. ② We replaced the weather stripping before winter.
