# 家居与日用 / 住宅 / 房贷保险与交易

[返回分类目录](../README.md)

1. **adjustable-rate mortgage**，读音：/ə.ˈdʒʌ.stə.bəl reɪt ˈmɔr.ɡədʒ/，重音：adjustable：主重音第2音节；rate：单音节；mortgage：主重音第1音节，中文：浮动利率房贷；口语：① How does an adjustable-rate mortgage work? ② I'm comparing a fixed-rate mortgage with an adjustable-rate mortgage.

2. **balloon payment**，读音：/bə.ˈlun ˈpeɪ.mənt/，重音：balloon：主重音第2音节；payment：主重音第1音节，中文：贷款期末一次性支付的大额尾款；口语：① How much is the final balloon payment? ② I didn't realize the loan had a balloon payment.

3. **closing costs**，读音：/ˈkloʊ.zɪŋ kɑsts/，重音：closing：主重音第1音节；costs：单音节，中文：交易费用；口语：① How much should we budget for closing costs? ② Are the closing costs included in that estimate?

4. **equity loan**，读音：/ˈɛ.kwə.ti loʊn/，重音：equity：主重音第1音节；loan：单音节，中文：房产净值贷款；口语：① Are you thinking about getting a home equity loan? ② I'd like to ask the bank about an equity loan.

5. **FHA**，读音：/ɛf eɪtʃ eɪ/，重音：按字母逐个读，通常末字母较重；实际缩写可能另有读法，中文：abbr. 联邦住宅管理局（Federal Housing Administration）；美国未来家庭主妇协会（Future Homemakers of America）；口语：① I saw a report from FHA. ② Have you checked the FHA website?

6. **fixed-rate mortgage**，读音：/fɪkst reɪt ˈmɔr.ɡədʒ/，重音：fixed：单音节；rate：单音节；mortgage：主重音第1音节，中文：固定利率房贷；口语：① I'd feel more comfortable with a fixed-rate mortgage. ② Did you choose a fixed-rate mortgage?

7. **homeowner insurance**，读音：/ˈhoʊ.ˌmoʊ.nɚ ˌɪn.ˈʃʊ.rəns/，重音：homeowner：主重音第1音节；insurance：主重音第2音节，中文：房屋保险；口语更自然：homeowners insurance；口语：① Do you mean homeowners insurance for the house? ② I need to compare homeowners insurance quotes.

8. **insurer**，读音：/ˌɪn.ˈʃʊ.rɚ/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：保险公司；口语：① Have you contacted your insurer yet? ② My insurer asked for a copy of the report.

9. **leasing**，读音：/ˈli.sɪŋ/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：说谎；口语：① [He's leasing his warehouse in Kabylie.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/8093935) ② [Gustavo reported Melissa to the leasing office.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/11769953)

10. **loan term**，读音：/loʊn tɝm/，重音：loan：单音节；term：单音节，中文：贷款期限；口语：① What's the loan term on that offer? ② Would a shorter loan term work for you?

11. **mortgage**，读音：/ˈmɔr.ɡədʒ/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：房贷；口语：① [I don't have enough money to pay the mortgage off.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/6385561) ② [I don't have a mortgage.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/4497899)

12. **mortgage lien**，读音：/ˈmɔr.ɡədʒ lin/，重音：mortgage：主重音第1音节；lien：单音节，中文：抵押权；口语：① Is there a mortgage lien on the property? ② The lawyer explained the mortgage lien to us.

13. **pmi (private mortgage insurance)**，读音：参考 /pˌiːˌɛmˈaɪ pɹˈaɪvət mˈɔːɹɡɪdʒ ɪnʃˈʊɹɹəns/，重音：ˈ后元音所在音节为主重音；ˌ为次重音，中文：私人房贷保险；口语：① Does that payment include PMI? ② When did you stop paying private mortgage insurance?

14. **pre-approval**，读音：/pri ə.ˈpru.vəl/，重音：pre：单音节；approval：主重音第2音节，中文：贷款预批准；口语：① That sounds pretty pre-approval to me. ② I found the whole thing rather pre-approval.

15. **pre-qualification**，读音：/pri ˌkwɑ.lə.fə.ˈkeɪ.ʃən/，重音：pre：单音节；qualification：主重音第4音节，中文：贷款初步资格；口语：① That sounds pretty pre-qualification to me. ② I found the whole thing rather pre-qualification.

16. **underwriting**，读音：/ˈʌn.dɚ.ˈraɪ.tɪŋ/，重音：主重音第1/3音节，中文：保险业；口语：① Can we talk about the underwriting for a minute? ② I still have a question about the underwriting.
