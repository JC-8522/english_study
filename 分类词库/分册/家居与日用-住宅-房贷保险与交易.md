# 家居与日用 / 住宅 / 房贷保险与交易

1. **adjustable-rate mortgage**，读音：/ə.ˈdʒʌ.stə.bəl reɪt ˈmɔr.ɡədʒ/，重音：adjustable：主重音第2音节；rate：单音节；mortgage：主重音第1音节，中文：浮动利率房贷；口语：① How does an adjustable-rate mortgage work? ② I'm comparing a fixed-rate mortgage with an adjustable-rate mortgage.

2. **balloon payment**，读音：/bə.ˈlun ˈpeɪ.mənt/，重音：balloon：主重音第2音节；payment：主重音第1音节，中文：贷款期末一次性支付的大额尾款；口语：① How much is the final balloon payment? ② I didn't realize the loan had a balloon payment.

3. **equity loan**，读音：/ˈɛ.kwə.ti loʊn/，重音：equity：主重音第1音节；loan：单音节，中文：房产净值贷款；口语：① Are you thinking about getting a home equity loan? ② I'd like to ask the bank about an equity loan.

4. **fixed-rate mortgage**，读音：/fɪkst reɪt ˈmɔr.ɡədʒ/，重音：fixed：单音节；rate：单音节；mortgage：主重音第1音节，中文：固定利率房贷；口语：① I'd feel more comfortable with a fixed-rate mortgage. ② Did you choose a fixed-rate mortgage?

5. **homeowner insurance**，读音：/ˈhoʊ.ˌmoʊ.nɚ ˌɪn.ˈʃʊ.rəns/，重音：homeowner：主重音第1音节；insurance：主重音第2音节，中文：房屋保险；口语更自然：homeowners insurance；口语：① Do you mean homeowners insurance for the house? ② I need to compare homeowners insurance quotes.

6. **interest rate**，读音：/ˈɪn.trəst reɪt/，重音：interest：主重音第1音节；rate：单音节，中文：利率；口语：① [What's the interest rate?](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/10188429) ② [Gradually the interest rate will increase.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/325332)

7. **loan term**，读音：/loʊn tɝm/，重音：loan：单音节；term：单音节，中文：贷款期限；口语：① What's the loan term on that offer? ② Would a shorter loan term work for you?

8. **mortgage lien**，读音：/ˈmɔr.ɡədʒ lin/，重音：mortgage：主重音第1音节；lien：单音节，中文：抵押权；口语：① Is there a mortgage lien on the property? ② The lawyer explained the mortgage lien to us.

9. **pmi (private mortgage insurance)**，读音：参考 /pˌiːˌɛmˈaɪ pɹˈaɪvət mˈɔːɹɡɪdʒ ɪnʃˈʊɹɹəns/，重音：ˈ后元音所在音节为主重音；ˌ为次重音，中文：私人房贷保险；口语：① Does that payment include PMI? ② When did you stop paying private mortgage insurance?

10. **pre-approval**，读音：/pri ə.ˈpru.vəl/，重音：pre：单音节；approval：主重音第2音节，中文：贷款预批准；口语：① That sounds pretty pre-approval to me. ② I found the whole thing rather pre-approval.

11. **pre-qualification**，读音：/pri ˌkwɑ.lə.fə.ˈkeɪ.ʃən/，重音：pre：单音节；qualification：主重音第4音节，中文：贷款初步资格；口语：① That sounds pretty pre-qualification to me. ② I found the whole thing rather pre-qualification.
