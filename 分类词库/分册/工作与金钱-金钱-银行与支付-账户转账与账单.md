# 工作与金钱 / 金钱 / 银行与支付 / 账户转账与账单

[返回分类目录](../README.md)

1. **autopay**，读音：/ˈɔ.toʊ.ˌpeɪ/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：自动扣款功能；口语：① I set up autopay for my phone bill. ② Can I turn off autopay for just this month?

2. **billed**，读音：/bɪld/，重音：单音节，中文：安排（bill的过去式与过去分词形式）；口语：① [The restaurant billed me 250,000 yen for the party.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/29515) ② [Jane was billed to appear as Ophelia.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/53813)

3. **chargeback**，读音：/ˈtʃɑrdʒ.ˌbæk/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：通过发卡机构提出争议后发起的款项撤回；口语：① Did the bank approve your chargeback? ② I asked my card issuer how to file a chargeback.

4. **credited**，读音：/ˈkrɛ.də.təd/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：相信；记入贷方（credit的过去式和过去分词）；口语：① [We hadn't credited this report.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/430203) ② [The government's economic policy is credited for Japan's postwar economic success.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/271418)

5. **direct deposit**，读音：/dɚ.ˈɛkt də.ˈpɑ.zɪt/，重音：可重读 deposit（主重音第2音节），中文：工资或款项直接存入银行账户；口语：① My direct deposit usually hits on Fridays. ② Who do I talk to about changing my direct deposit?

6. **overdraft**，读音：/ˈoʊ.vɚ.ˌdræft/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：透支，账户支出超过可用余额；口语：① I got charged an overdraft fee. ② Does this account have overdraft protection?

7. **past due**，读音：/pæst ˈdu/，重音：可重读 due，中文：已过付款或交付期限；口语：① My water bill says it's past due. ② Is this balance past due or just coming up?

8. **payable**，读音：/ˈpeɪ.ə.bəl/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：可付的；到期的；口语：① [This check is payable to the bearer.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/58501) ② [Checks should be made payable to the ABC Company.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/2290598)

9. **payouts**，读音：/ˈpeɪ.ˌaʊts/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：(payout 的复数) n. 支出；付出款项；口语：① Can we talk about the payouts for a minute? ② I still have a question about the payouts.

10. **pending charge**，读音：/ˈpɛn.dɪŋ tʃɑrdʒ/，重音：可重读 pending（主重音第1音节），中文：尚未正式入账的扣款；口语：① There's a pending charge from the hotel. ② That pending charge showed up twice on my card.

11. **prepaid**，读音：/pri.ˈpeɪd/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：先付的；口语：① [Sami's taxi was prepaid.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/7245598) ② [Every Christmas, my brother gifts me a prepaid Visa card.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/12457389)

12. **put down a deposit**，读音：/pʊt daʊn ə də.ˈpɑ.zɪt/，重音：可重读 deposit（主重音第2音节），中文：先付一笔押金或订金；口语：① We put down a deposit on the couch. ② Do I need to put down a deposit to hold the room?

13. **refunds**，读音：/rɪ.ˈfʌndz/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：归还；偿还额；口语：① [We do not offer refunds on any item.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/2740149) ② [We don't do refunds.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/8058320)

14. **split the bill**，读音：/splɪt ðə ˈbɪl/，重音：可重读 bill，中文：一起分摊账单；口语：① Do you want to split the bill? ② We split the bill three ways.

15. **unpaid**，读音：/ən.ˈpeɪd/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：未付款的；不支薪水的；口语：① [I gave up on receiving the unpaid salary.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/1534971) ② [I have a lot of unpaid parking tickets.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/6660059)
