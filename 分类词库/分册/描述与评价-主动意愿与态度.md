# 描述与评价 / 主动意愿与态度

[返回分类目录](../README.md)

1. **apt**，读音：/æpt/，重音：单音节，中文：有...倾向的；易于的；口语：① [We are apt to speak ill of others.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/263131) ② [We're apt to make mistakes.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/7803591)

2. **convinced**，读音：/kən.ˈvɪnst/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：确信的；深信的；口语：① [I'm not convinced we share the same goal.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/12249842) ② [What convinced you Tom and Mary weren't guilty?](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/6506746)

3. **counterintuitive**，读音：/,kajntәrinˈtju:itiv/，重音：主重音在ˈ之后的音节；ˌ为次重音，中文：反直觉的；口语：① [It's counterintuitive, but true.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/5729044) ② [Reality is often counterintuitive.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/9265866)

4. **inclined**，读音：/ˌɪn.ˈklaɪnd/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：倾向于...的；想要...的；口语：① [I'm inclined to believe Tom is a spy.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/10208974) ② [I don't feel inclined to play baseball today.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/242982)

5. **intuitive**，读音：/ˌɪn.ˈtu.ə.tɪv/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：有直觉的；口语：① [I'm a very intuitive person.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/6178806) ② [I know English on an intuitive level.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/2946770)

6. **proactive**，读音：/ˌproʊ.ˈæk.tɪv/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：〈心理〉前摄的；主动的；口语：① [We're being very proactive.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/5737640) ② [I'm proactive in shaping my reality, rather than being a passive observer.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/12257313)

7. **unintentional**，读音：/ˌʌ.nɪn.ˈtɛn.ʃə.nəl/，重音：主重音第3音节，中文：非故意的；口语：① [I didn't do anything unintentional.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/12999112) ② [The company claims that the delay was unintentional.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/10733933)

8. **unsolicited**，读音：/ˌʌn.sə.ˈlɪ.sɪ.tɪd/，重音：主重音第3音节，中文：未经请求的；主动提供的；口语：① [We are not currently taking unsolicited submissions.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/11570909) ② [Can I give you some unsolicited advice?](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/3329768)
