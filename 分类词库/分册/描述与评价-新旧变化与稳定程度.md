9. **sustainability**，读音：/sə.ˌsteɪ.nə.ˈbɪ.lɪ.ti/，重音：主重音第4音节，中文：持续性；能维持性；口语：① [What can we learn from other cultures about sustainability?](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/12768077) ② [Mary's garden is a blend of beauty and sustainability.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/12767995)
