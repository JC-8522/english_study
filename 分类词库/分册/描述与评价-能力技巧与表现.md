# 描述与评价 / 能力技巧与表现

[返回分类目录](../README.md)

1. **abilities**，读音：/ə.ˈbɪ.lə.ˌtiz/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：能力；才能（ability的复数形式）；口语：① [I don't have much belief in his abilities.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/260626) ② [I've always been impressed by your abilities.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/3735784)

2. **adept**，读音：/ə.ˈdɛpt/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：很擅长某项技能的；口语：① She's adept at calming people down. ② He got pretty adept at fixing bikes.

3. **aptitude**，读音：/ˈæp.tə.ˌtud/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：学习或从事某事的自然能力；口语：① She's always had an aptitude for languages. ② He has an aptitude for working with numbers.

4. **characterization**，读音：/ˌkɛ.rək.tɚ.ɪ.ˈzeɪ.ʃən/，重音：主重音第5音节，中文：特性描述；人物之塑造；口语：① [I don't think that's a very accurate characterization of his point of view.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/13627706) ② [The theater company's characterization of Anne Frank was very emotional, but tasteful.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/793698)

5. **clever**，读音：/ˈklɛ.vɚ/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：聪明的；精明的；口语：① [I know Tom isn't a very clever boy.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/7518410) ② [I'd like to be as clever as him.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/13849264)

6. **competence**，读音：/ˈkɑm.pə.tɪns/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：胜任；职称；口语：① [I'm not questioning your competence.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/2963476) ② [Ted doesn't have the competence to teach English.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/39437)

7. **competencies**，读音：/ˈkɑm.pə.tɪn.siz/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：能力（competency的复数）；胜任特征；口语：① Can we talk about the competencies for a minute? ② I still have a question about the competencies.

8. **competent**，读音：/ˈkɑm.pə.tɪnt/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：能干的；胜任的；口语：① [I'm sure your German teacher is very competent.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/8188227) ② [I've been told that he's a very competent person.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/2258892)

9. **efficient**，读音：/ɪ.ˈfɪ.ʃənt/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：高效的；口语：① [I'm very efficient when there is nothing to do.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/9506437) ② [We take particular pride in providing efficient solutions.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/1578987)

10. **eminent**，读音：/ˈɛ.mə.nənt/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：显赫的；杰出的；口语：① [They took the abandoned houses through eminent domain.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/8983398) ② [He became an eminent figure in modern philosophy.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/13748327)

11. **illustrious**，读音：/ˌɪ.ˈlʌ.stri.əs/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：杰出的；著名的；口语：① [Our illustrious leader wrote those words before he rose to power.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/9032601) ② [Massinissa was the most illustrious king of Numidia.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/11844206)

12. **keen**，读音：/kin/，重音：单音节，中文：锋利的；敏锐的；口语：① [I'm not keen on this kind of music.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/321604) ② [I'm not so keen on vegetables like these.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/8777534)

13. **knack**，读音：/næk/，重音：单音节，中文：做某事的窍门或天赋；口语：① She's got a knack for fixing things. ② I never got the knack of doing that by hand.

14. **knowledgeable**，读音：/ˈnɑ.lə.dʒə.bəl/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：知识渊博的；口语：① [You seem to be very knowledgeable about this.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/5936570) ② [I'm knowledgeable, but not skilled.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/6029574)

15. **mediocre**，读音：/ˌmi.di.ˈoʊ.kɚ/，重音：主重音第3音节，中文：平庸的；普通而不出色的；口语：① [I don't want to live a mediocre life.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/8260039) ② [We're not afraid to be mediocre.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/8984796)

16. **practiced**，读音：/ˈpræk.təst/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：有经验的；熟练的；口语：① [I haven't yet practiced the piano today.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/6713312) ② [I haven't practiced the piano in a long time.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/6713313)

17. **proficiency**，读音：/prə.ˈfɪ.ʃən.si/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：精通；熟练；口语：① [Billy made hurtful jokes about the foreign student's English proficiency.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/11768370) ② [He has increased his proficiency in English greatly.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/302973)

18. **proficient**，读音：/prɑ.ˈfɪ.ʃənt/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：高手；专家；口语：① [I'm quite proficient in first aid.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/11005598) ② [You won't become proficient if you only study the things you enjoy.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/9993313)

19. **prominent**，读音：/ˈprɑ.mə.nənt/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：卓越的；显著的；口语：① [He's from a prominent Algerian family.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/8303646) ② [He's famous as a prominent pianist.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/8763561)

20. **ripe**，读音：/raɪp/，重音：单音节，中文：成熟的；熟练的；口语：① [I'll leave the bananas alone until they're ripe.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/12589173) ② [Have you ever eaten a ripe Barbados cherry?](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/6477354)

21. **skilled**，读音：/skɪld/，重音：单音节，中文：熟练的；口语：① [You're the least skilled liar in the world.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/12804351) ② [I'm skilled at coming up with fun ideas.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/12982790)

22. **slick**，读音：/slɪk/，重音：单音节，中文：光滑的；熟练的；口语：① [I'll just slick my hair back.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/7091312) ② [Don't swallow that slick propaganda.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/50652)

23. **sophisticated**，读音：/sə.ˈfɪ.stə.ˌkeɪ.tɪd/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：复杂的；久经世故的；精致的；口语：① [I think it's a little more sophisticated than that.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/3137191) ② [You think I'm not as sophisticated as you, don't you?](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/3137168)
