# 描述与评价 / 能力技巧与表现

1. **abilities**，读音：/ə.ˈbɪ.lə.ˌtiz/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：能力；才能（ability的复数形式）；口语：① [I don't have much belief in his abilities.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/260626) ② [I've always been impressed by your abilities.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/3735784)

2. **characterization**，读音：/ˌkɛ.rək.tɚ.ɪ.ˈzeɪ.ʃən/，重音：主重音第5音节，中文：特性描述；人物之塑造；口语：① [I don't think that's a very accurate characterization of his point of view.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/13627706) ② [The theater company's characterization of Anne Frank was very emotional, but tasteful.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/793698)

4. **competencies**，读音：/ˈkɑm.pə.tɪn.siz/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：能力（competency的复数）；胜任特征；口语：① Can we talk about the competencies for a minute? ② I still have a question about the competencies.

5. **competent**，读音：/ˈkɑm.pə.tɪnt/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：能干的；胜任的；口语：① [I'm sure your German teacher is very competent.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/8188227) ② [I've been told that he's a very competent person.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/2258892)

7. **eminent**，读音：/ˈɛ.mə.nənt/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：显赫的；杰出的；口语：① [They took the abandoned houses through eminent domain.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/8983398) ② [He became an eminent figure in modern philosophy.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/13748327)

8. **illustrious**，读音：/ˌɪ.ˈlʌ.stri.əs/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：杰出的；著名的；口语：① [Our illustrious leader wrote those words before he rose to power.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/9032601) ② [Massinissa was the most illustrious king of Numidia.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/11844206)

9. **mediocre**，读音：/ˌmi.di.ˈoʊ.kɚ/，重音：主重音第3音节，中文：平庸的；普通而不出色的；口语：① [I don't want to live a mediocre life.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/8260039) ② [We're not afraid to be mediocre.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/8984796)

11. **proficiency**，读音：/prə.ˈfɪ.ʃən.si/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：精通；熟练；口语：① [Billy made hurtful jokes about the foreign student's English proficiency.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/11768370) ② [He has increased his proficiency in English greatly.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/302973)
