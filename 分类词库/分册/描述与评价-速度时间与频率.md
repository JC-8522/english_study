# 描述与评价 / 速度时间与频率

1. **consecutive**，读音：/kən.ˈsɛk.jə.tɪv/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：连续的；联贯的；口语：① [Have you ever felt depressed for most of the day for 14 consecutive days?](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/9851474) ② [They've won for the third consecutive year.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/10541993)

3. **cyclic**，读音：/ˈsaɪ.klɪk/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：周期的；轮转的；口语：① [Life's nature is cyclic.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/1143479) ② [This is a cyclic topic.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/868123)

5. **fast**，读音：/fæst/，重音：单音节，中文：快速的；口语：① [I didn't do that very fast, did I?](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/6739976) ② [You know what fast food is, don't you?](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/8869275)

8. **occasional**，读音：/ə.ˈkeɪ.ʒə.nəl/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：偶然的；临时的；口语：① [I prefer vodka myself, though I don't mind the occasional liqueur.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/2000286) ② [I have occasional pains in the stomach.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/258149)

9. **quick**，读音：/kwɪk/，重音：单音节，中文：快速的；迅速的；口语：① [I think I'll just have a quick bite.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/2207442) ② [I'll be there as quick as I can.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/2952727)

10. **rapid**，读音：/ˈræ.pəd/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：急速的；快速的；口语：① [I'm amazed at his rapid progress in English.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/285555) ② [Can we check the rapid spread of the 'flu?](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/22634)
