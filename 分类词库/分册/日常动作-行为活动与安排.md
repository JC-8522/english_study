1. **ban**，读音：/bæn/，重音：单音节，中文：禁令；口语：① [Let us not ban everything we disagree with.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/5927291) ② [We must ban the Russian language on Tatoeba.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/10688812)

2. **bans**，读音：/bænz/，重音：单音节，中文：结婚预告（等于banns）；口语：① [A society that bans religious headgear is not free.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/11044190) ② [The treaty bans the use of chemical weapons.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/268526)

3. **closeout**，读音：/ˈkləʊzaʊt/，重音：主重音在ˈ之后的音节；ˌ为次重音，中文：出清存货；出清存货；口语：① Can we talk about the closeout for a minute? ② I still have a question about the closeout.

4. **closeouts**，读音：/ˈkləʊzˌaʊts/，重音：主重音在ˈ之后的音节；ˌ为次重音，中文：出清存货，出清存货( closeout的名词复数 )；口语：① Can we talk about the closeouts for a minute? ② I still have a question about the closeouts.

5. **fiat**，读音：/ˈfaɪ.æt/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：命令；严命；口语：① [The current college works by presidential fiat.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/1354417) ② [It's a fiat currency.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/8636538)
