1. **afterwards**，读音：/ˈæf.tɚ.wɚdz/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：然后；后来；口语：① [I don't know what happened afterwards.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/8424642) ② [I'll come and meet you afterwards.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/9082324)
