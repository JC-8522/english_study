# 语言与表达 / 日常口语 / 简写与非正式拼写

[返回分类目录](../README.md)

1. **alot**，读音：/ə.ˈlɑt/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：(Alot)人名；(西)阿洛特；口语：① Can we talk about the alot for a minute? ② I still have a question about the alot.

2. **cant**，读音：/kænt/，重音：单音节，中文：伪善之言；黑话；口语：① [Ganovim-loshn was a Yiddish thieves' cant.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/10463297) ② [Gay people in Britain used to speak a cant called Polari.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/10352307)

3. **cuz**，读音：/kәz/，重音：单音节或按短语自然重读，中文：abbr. 因为（cause）；口语：① Can we talk about the cuz for a minute? ② I still have a question about the cuz.

4. **didnt**，读音：参考 /dˈɪdnt/，重音：ˈ后元音所在音节为主重音；ˌ为次重音，中文：didn't 的非标准写法，did not 的缩写；口语：① [He didnt get the job.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/12727505) ② I didn't see your message until this morning.

5. **doesnt**，读音：参考 /dˈʌzənt/，重音：ˈ后元音所在音节为主重音；ˌ为次重音，中文：doesn't 的非标准写法，does not 的缩写；口语：① [He is pretending that he doesnt know us whats up with him?](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/12587323) ② She doesn't work on Fridays.

6. **dunno**，读音：/də.ˈnoʊ/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：&lt;口&gt;(我)不知道；口语：① [I dunno what's with ya these days.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/2015964) ② [I dunno if it's a bug or what, but this software doesn't work right.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/459808)

7. **expressing**，读音：/ɪk.ˈsprɛ.sɪŋ/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：表达( express的现在分词 )；（用符号等）表示；口语：① [I'm not good at expressing my feelings.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/258272) ② [I'm documenting language use, not expressing opinions.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/14058673)

8. **fyi**，读音：/ˈfɔr.ˈjɔ.ˌrɪn.fɚ.ˈmeɪ.ʃən/，重音：主重音第1/2/5音节，中文：供参考；口语：① Can we talk about the fyi for a minute? ② I still have a question about the fyi.

9. **gotta**，读音：/ˈɡɑ.tə/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：必须；有；口语：① [I gotta figure out what I'm doing wrong.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/5213247) ② [I've gotta go up and perform now.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/4638951)

10. **IMO**，读音：/ˈaɪ.moʊ/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：abbr. imitation (slang short form) 模仿；冒充（短形式俚语）；口语：① I keep seeing "IMO" in this document. ② Can you explain what "IMO" means here?

11. **isnt**，读音：参考 /ˈɪsnt/，重音：ˈ后元音所在音节为主重音；ˌ为次重音，中文：abbr. 原位缺口平移（In Situ Nick Translation）；口语：① Can we talk about the isnt for a minute? ② I still have a question about the isnt.

12. **nope**，读音：/noʊp/，重音：单音节，中文：不；不是；口语：① [I think I'm gonna nope out of this one.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/9722839) ② [Nope, I'm not buying it.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/5350456)

13. **pooh**，读音：/pu/，重音：单音节，中文：呸；啐；口语：① Can we talk about the pooh for a minute? ② I still have a question about the pooh.

14. **rockin**，读音：参考 /ɹˈɑːkɪn/，重音：ˈ后元音所在音节为主重音；ˌ为次重音，中文：rocking 的非正式拼写；很棒的、带劲的（依语境）；口语：① Can we talk about the rockin for a minute? ② I still have a question about the rockin.

15. **Tara**，读音：/ˈtɛ.rə/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：int. \[口\]再会！；口语：① We're planning a trip to Tara. ② Have you ever been to Tara?

16. **thanx**，读音：参考 /θˈæŋks/，重音：ˈ后元音所在音节为主重音；ˌ为次重音，中文：thanks 的非正式拼写：谢谢；口语：① Can we talk about the thanx for a minute? ② I still have a question about the thanx.

17. **thats**，读音：参考 /ðˈæts/，重音：ˈ后元音所在音节为主重音；ˌ为次重音，中文：那；那个；口语：① Can we talk about the thats for a minute? ② I still have a question about the thats.

18. **THO**，读音：/ðoʊ/，重音：单音节，中文：abbr. 虽然；尽管；口语：① I keep seeing "THO" in this document. ② Can you explain what "THO" means here?

19. **thru**，读音：/θru/，重音：单音节，中文：穿越；从头至尾；口语：① I sent it thru the app. ② Can we use the drive-thru on the way home?

20. **TIA**，读音：/ˈti.ə/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：abbr. 先谢了（Thanks In Advance）；口语：① I keep seeing "TIA" in this document. ② Can you explain what "TIA" means here?

21. **wanna**，读音：/ˈwɑ.nə/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：要(做)；想(做)；口语：① [I don't wanna hear another word about it.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/2724644) ② [I don't wanna hear a word from you.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/3292270)

22. **yea**，读音：/jeɪ/，重音：单音节，中文：肯定；赞成；口语：① [Yea, that's all it is.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/523614) ② [Yea, I guess you can call that a supermarket.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/5607367)

23. **yep**，读音：/jɛp/，重音：单音节，中文：是（美国俚语=yes）；口语：① [Yep, go to Tatoeba and write it there!](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/8887763) ② [Yep, that's the case.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/13825088)

24. **yer**，读音：/jɝ/，重音：单音节，中文：\[俚\]你的（等于your）；口语：① [Everybody shut yer traps!](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/3859322) ② Can we talk about the yer for a minute?

25. **yup**，读音：/jʌp/，重音：单音节，中文：int. \[俚\]是的（等于yes）；口语：① [Yup, I am angry.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/4061547) ② [Yup, it seems like even the doctor was about to throw in the towel.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/1787076)
