# 语言与表达 / 说话交流与表达 / 询问回答与澄清

1. **advised**，读音：/æd.ˈvaɪzd/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：考虑过的；知情的；口语：① [I would've advised Tom not to do that.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/6242894) ② [I should've advised Tom to keep it secret.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/6664517)

2. **answered**，读音：/ˈæn.sɚd/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：回答；回复（answer的过去式）；口语：① [I have a question I'd like answered first.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/2358795) ② [I still haven't answered all of Tom's questions.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/6234014)

3. **answering**，读音：/ˈæn.sɚ.ɪŋ/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：答案；回答；口语：① [Why aren't you answering me as of lately?](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/12601515) ② [I'm having a hard time answering your question.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/2798712)

4. **ask a follow-up question**，读音：/æsk ə ˈfɑ.loʊ ʌp ˈkwɛs.tʃən/，重音：可重读 question（主重音第1音节），中文：根据对方刚才说的话继续问一个问题；口语：① Can I ask a follow-up question? ② She asked a follow-up question about parking.

5. **cited**，读音：/ˈsaɪ.təd/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：引用（cite的过去分词）；口语：① [We were cited for breach of peace.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/6865520) ② [Tom could've been cited for that.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/7127388)

6. **clarification**，读音：/ˌklɛ.rə.fə.ˈkeɪ.ʃən/，重音：主重音第4音节，中文：澄清；净化；口语：① [We are at your disposal for any further clarification.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/10841553) ② [I need more clarification on this matter.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/13668360)

7. **clarified**，读音：/ˈklɛ.rə.ˌfaɪd/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：使清楚；澄清( clarify的过去式和过去分词 )；口语：① [I just had to get that clarified.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/8853234) ② [We have already clarified this with you.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/6365824)

8. **clarify**，读音：/ˈklɛ.rə.ˌfaɪ/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：澄清；阐明；口语：① [You guys didn't clarify anything for him.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/7841396) ② [Do you need me to clarify anything further?](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/13222047)

9. **clarifying**，读音：/ˈklɛ.rə.ˌfaɪ.ɪŋ/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：澄清性的；口语：① [Would you mind clarifying that last part for me?](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/13247215) ② [I appreciate your help in clarifying this.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/59458)

10. **clear up a misunderstanding**，读音：/klɪr ʌp ə ˌmɪ.sən.dɚ.ˈstæn.dɪŋ/，重音：可重读 misunderstanding（主重音第4音节），中文：澄清一场误会；口语：① Can we talk and clear up the misunderstanding? ② I called to clear up a misunderstanding about the time.

11. **clearer**，读音：/ˈklɪ.rɚ/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：\[医\] 澄清剂, 透明剂; 洗剂(牙)；\[经\] (票据)交换员；口语：① [I can't make it any clearer than that.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/1954630) ② [Can it get any clearer than this?](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/12771149)

12. **double-check with**，读音：/ˈdʌ.bəl ˈtʃɛk wɪð/，重音：可重读 check，中文：找某人再核实一遍；口语：① Can you double-check with the front desk? ② I want to double-check with Sam before we book it.

13. **enquire**，读音：/ɪn.ˈkwaɪ.ɚ/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：询问；口语：① [Why don't you telephone the theatre and enquire about tickets?](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/939300) ② [I'll now take further steps to enquire as to how that mistake occurred.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/11008049)

14. **enquiries**，读音：/inˈkwaiəriz/，重音：主重音在ˈ之后的音节；ˌ为次重音，中文：询问（ enquiry的复数形式 ）；调查；口语：① [Serious enquiries only, please.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/11578447) ② [Police are conducting enquiries into what caused the train to leave the tracks.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/2978742)

15. **enquiry**，读音：/ɪn.ˈkwaɪ.ˌri/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：询问；口语：① [I appreciate your answering my enquiry so promptly.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/1590805) ② [The long-awaited commission of enquiry report was released yesterday afternoon.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/3222485)

16. **get clarification on**，读音：/ɡɛt ˌklɛ.rə.fə.ˈkeɪ.ʃən ɑn/，重音：可重读 clarification（主重音第4音节），中文：就某事请对方进一步说明；口语：① I need to get clarification on the deadline. ② Did you get clarification on what the fee covers?

17. **get the wrong idea**，读音：/ɡɛt ðə rɔŋ aɪ.ˈdi.ə/，重音：可重读 idea（主重音第2音节），中文：误解某件事或别人的意思；口语：① I don't want you to get the wrong idea. ② I got the wrong idea from your text.

18. **get your wires crossed**，读音：/ɡɛt jɚ ˈwaɪ.ɚz ˈkrɔst/，重音：可重读 crossed，中文：沟通中彼此误会，理解成了不同的事；口语：① I think we got our wires crossed about the time. ② Sorry, we must've gotten our wires crossed.

19. **go over something again**，读音：/ɡoʊ ˈoʊ.vɚ ˈsʌm.θɪŋ ə.ˈɡɛn/，重音：可重读 again（主重音第2音节），中文：把某件事再讲或再核对一遍；口语：① Can we go over that part again? ② We went over the instructions again before we left.

20. **inquire**，读音：/ˌɪn.ˈkwaɪr/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：询问；问明；口语：① [Let's inquire about our hotel reservations.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/33377) ② [Did you inquire as to whether you should leave?](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/873003)

21. **interrogate**，读音：/ˌɪn.ˈtɛ.rə.ˌɡeɪt/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：审问；盘问；深入质疑；口语：① [I was aware they would interrogate me.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/12677350) ② [Did you interrogate them?](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/7132098)

22. **investigating**，读音：/ˌɪn.ˈvɛ.stə.ˌɡeɪ.tɪŋ/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：调查；审查；口语：① [We're investigating the deaths of Tom and Mary.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/3730063) ② [I assume that you'll be investigating this.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/7190644)

23. **justify**，读音：/ˈdʒʌ.stə.ˌfaɪ/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：替...辩护；证明；证明...合理；口语：① [I don't know if I can justify it.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/10333816) ② [I don't feel any need to justify this position.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/10092443)

24. **pick someone's brain**，读音：/pɪk ˈsʌm.ˌwʌnz ˈbreɪn/，重音：可重读 brain，中文：向某人请教经验或想法；口语：① Can I pick your brain about buying a used car? ② I picked her brain about what to expect in the interview.
