4. **cited**，读音：/ˈsaɪ.təd/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：引用（cite的过去分词）；口语：① [We were cited for breach of peace.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/6865520) ② [Tom could've been cited for that.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/7127388)

6. **clarified**，读音：/ˈklɛ.rə.ˌfaɪd/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：使清楚；澄清( clarify的过去式和过去分词 )；口语：① [I just had to get that clarified.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/8853234) ② [We have already clarified this with you.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/6365824)

7. **clarify**，读音：/ˈklɛ.rə.ˌfaɪ/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：澄清；阐明；口语：① [You guys didn't clarify anything for him.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/7841396) ② [Do you need me to clarify anything further?](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/13222047)

8. **clarifying**，读音：/ˈklɛ.rə.ˌfaɪ.ɪŋ/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：澄清性的；口语：① [Would you mind clarifying that last part for me?](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/13247215) ② [I appreciate your help in clarifying this.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/59458)

9. **clearer**，读音：/ˈklɪ.rɚ/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：\[医\] 澄清剂, 透明剂; 洗剂(牙)；\[经\] (票据)交换员；口语：① [I can't make it any clearer than that.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/1954630) ② [Can it get any clearer than this?](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/12771149)
