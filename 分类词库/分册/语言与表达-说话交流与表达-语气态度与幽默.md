# 语言与表达 / 说话交流与表达 / 语气态度与幽默

[返回分类目录](../README.md)

1. **banter**，读音：/ˈbæn.tɚ/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：轻松的打趣和玩笑；口语：① I like the banter between those two hosts. ② It was just friendly banter.

2. **cheesy**，读音：/ˈtʃi.zi/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：俗套、肉麻或刻意煽情的；口语：① That ending was a little cheesy. ② I love a cheesy joke once in a while.

3. **chuckle**，读音：/ˈtʃʌ.kəl/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：低声轻笑，也指这类笑声；口语：① She chuckled when she heard the story. ② That photo always gets a chuckle out of me.

4. **come across as**，读音：/kʌm ə.ˈkrɔs æz/，重音：可重读 across（主重音第2音节），中文：给人留下某种印象；口语：① I hope I didn't come across as rude. ② He comes across as quiet at first.

5. **complimentary**，读音：/ˌkɑm.plə.ˈmɛn.tɚ.i/，重音：主重音第3音节，中文：问候的；称赞的；口语：① [Please enjoy our complimentary towels.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/5293172) ② [First class plane flights come with complimentary alcohol.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/2334863)

6. **corny**，读音：/ˈkɔr.ni/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：老套、土气或故作感人的，常形容笑话；口语：① My dad tells the corniest jokes. ② I know it sounds corny, but I really mean it.

7. **deadpan**，读音：/ˈdɛd.ˌpæn/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：面无表情、一本正经的，可形容说笑时的语气；口语：① She said it in such a deadpan voice that I thought she was serious. ② His deadpan delivery makes it even funnier.

8. **dry sense of humor**，读音：/draɪ sɛns əv ˈhju.mɚ/，重音：可重读 humor（主重音第1音节），中文：用平淡语气说出笑点的幽默感；口语：① She has a dry sense of humor, so it takes a second to get the joke. ② My dad has the driest sense of humor.

9. **give someone a look**，读音：/ɡɪv ˈsʌm.ˌwʌn ə ˈlʊk/，重音：可重读 look，中文：用一个眼神向某人表达态度；口语：① My sister gave me a look, so I stopped talking. ② He gave me a look when I reached for the last slice.

10. **gushed**，读音：/ɡʌʃt/，重音：单音节，中文：滔滔不绝地热情说；涌出；口语：① [Ziri gushed out about how much he liked Rima.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/10771781) ② [Water gushed out of the faucet.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/12471993)

11. **ironic**，读音：/aɪ.ˈrɑ.nɪk/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：冷嘲的；挖苦的；讽刺意味的；口语：① [I don't like her ironic smile at all.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/13570196) ② [I'm not sure if this is ironic or not.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/2485153)

12. **josh**，读音：/dʒɑʃ/，重音：单音节，中文：戏弄；说笑；口语：① [Rogan josh may be the most famous recipe of Kashmiri cuisine.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/8689329) ② [Josh is cooking tapsilog.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/11716436)

13. **keep a straight face**，读音：/kip ə streɪt ˈfeɪs/，重音：可重读 face，中文：忍住笑，保持一本正经；口语：① I couldn't keep a straight face when he said that. ② How did you keep a straight face through the whole thing?

14. **lighten the mood**，读音：/ˈlaɪ.tən ðə ˈmud/，重音：可重读 mood，中文：让紧张或沉闷的气氛轻松一点；口语：① I told a silly story to lighten the mood. ② Some music might help lighten the mood.

15. **offhand remark**，读音：/ˈɔf.ˈhænd rɪ.ˈmɑrk/，重音：可重读 offhand（主重音第1/2音节），中文：随口说的一句话；口语：① It was an offhand remark, but it stuck with me. ② He made an offhand remark about moving to Denver.

16. **play it off**，读音：/pleɪ ɪt ˈɔf/，重音：可重读 off，中文：装作刚才的尴尬或失误没什么大不了；口语：① I called her by the wrong name and tried to play it off. ② He tripped on the step and played it off like he meant to do it.

17. **praised**，读音：/preɪzd/，重音：单音节，中文：称赞( praise的过去式和过去分词 )；赞美；口语：① [You will be praised for speaking the truth.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/2154797) ② [I shouldn't have praised you.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/9014948)

18. **read the room**，读音：/rid ðə ˈrum/，重音：可重读 room，中文：察觉现场气氛，判断此时适合说什么；口语：① Read the room. Nobody's in the mood for jokes right now. ② She read the room and changed the subject.

19. **rub someone the wrong way**，读音：/rʌb ˈsʌm.ˌwʌn ðə rɔŋ ˈweɪ/，重音：可重读 way，中文：让某人觉得不舒服或反感；口语：① His tone rubbed me the wrong way. ② I can see why that comment rubbed you the wrong way.

20. **sarcasm**，读音：/ˈsɑr.ˌkæ.zəm/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：常通过反着说来表达的讽刺或挖苦；口语：① I couldn't tell if that was sarcasm. ② My sarcasm doesn't always come across in texts.

21. **self-deprecating**，读音：/sɛlf ˈdɛ.prə.ˌkeɪ.tɪŋ/，重音：可重读 deprecating（主重音第1音节），中文：自嘲的，拿自己的缺点或尴尬事开玩笑的；口语：① He's got a very self-deprecating sense of humor. ② Her self-deprecating jokes made everyone feel at ease.

22. **snarky**，读音：/ˈsnɑr.ki/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：说话带挖苦、尖刻感的；口语：① I didn't mean to sound snarky. ② That was a pretty snarky reply.

23. **wisecrack**，读音：/ˈwaɪz.ˌkræk/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：俏皮或挖苦的一句玩笑；口语：① He made a wisecrack about my cooking. ② Save the wisecracks until after the meeting.

24. **without missing a beat**，读音：/wɪ.ˈθaʊt ˈmɪ.sɪŋ ə ˈbit/，重音：可重读 beat，中文：毫无停顿地继续或作出反应；口语：① She answered without missing a beat. ② The mic went out, and he kept talking without missing a beat.
