# 饮食 / 酒 / 啤酒与酿造

[返回分类目录](../README.md) · [完整清单](../active%20words.md)

1. **ale**，读音：/eɪl/，重音：单音节，中文：麦芽酒；口语：① What ales do you have on tap? ② I'll try the local ale.

2. **beers**，读音：/bɪrz/，重音：单音节，中文：比尔斯（姓氏）；口语：① Can we get two beers, please? ② What local beers do you recommend?

3. **brew**，读音：/bru/，重音：单音节，中文：酿造酒；酝酿；口语：① They brew their own beer here. ② Can I try your latest brew?

4. **brewer**，读音：/ˈbru.ɚ/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：(啤酒)制造者；泡制饮料的器皿；口语：① My friend works as a brewer. ② Let's ask the brewer about this beer.

5. **brewers**，读音：/ˈbru.ɚz/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：啤酒制造者( brewer的复数形式 )；口语：① The brewers are trying a new recipe. ② We met the brewers at the festival.

6. **brewery**，读音：/ˈbru.ɚ.i/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：啤酒厂；酿酒厂；口语：① There's a small brewery near our hotel. ② Do you want to take a brewery tour?

7. **guinness**，读音：/ˈɡɪ.nəs/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：健力士；吉尼斯（此处指爱尔兰黑啤酒品牌）；口语：① I'll have a pint of Guinness, please. ② Do you have Guinness on tap?

8. **stein**，读音：/staɪn/，重音：单音节，中文：啤酒杯；口语：① He brought his own beer stein. ② Can I get this beer in a stein?

9. **stout**，读音：/staʊt/，重音：单音节，中文：结实的；矮胖的；丰满的 / 结实的；口语：① I'll have a pint of stout, please. ② Do you prefer stout or lager?
