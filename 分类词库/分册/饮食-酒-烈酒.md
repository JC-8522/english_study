# 饮食 / 酒 / 烈酒

[返回分类目录](../README.md)

1. **brandy**，读音：/ˈbræn.di/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：白兰地酒；口语：① Would you like a little brandy after dinner? ② He bought a bottle of brandy for his dad.

2. **gin**，读音：/dʒɪn/，重音：单音节，中文：杜松子酒；轧棉机；口语：① I'll have a gin and tonic, please. ② Is there gin in this cocktail?

3. **liquor**，读音：/ˈlɪ.kɚ/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：酒；液体；口语：① Is there a liquor store near here? ② I don't usually drink hard liquor.

4. **rum**，读音：/rʌm/，重音：单音节，中文：朗姆酒；口语：① Is this made with white rum or dark rum? ② I added a little rum to the cake.

5. **spirits**，读音：/ˈspɪ.rɪts/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：烈酒；精神、情绪（另义）；口语：① Do you sell spirits as well as wine? ② I usually stick to beer rather than spirits.

6. **vodka**，读音：/ˈvɑd.kə/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：伏特加酒；口语：① I'll have a vodka and soda, please. ② Is there vodka in this drink?

7. **whiskey**，读音：/ˈwɪ.ski/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：威士忌酒；口语：① He ordered a whiskey on the rocks. ② Would you like a little water in your whiskey?

8. **whisky**，读音：/ˈwɪ.ski/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：威士忌酒；轻便马车；口语：① I'd like to try a Scottish whisky. ② She bought a bottle of whisky as a gift.
