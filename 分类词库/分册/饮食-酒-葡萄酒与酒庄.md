# 饮食 / 酒 / 葡萄酒与酒庄

[返回分类目录](../README.md)

1. **bordeaux**，读音：/bɔr.ˈdoʊ/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：波尔多葡萄酒；口语：① We opened a bottle of Bordeaux with dinner. ② Do you have a Bordeaux by the glass?

2. **burgundy**，读音：/ˈbɝ.ɡən.di/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：勃艮第(法国一地区)；勃艮第葡萄酒；口语：① We had a bottle of Burgundy with dinner. ② Do you prefer Burgundy or Bordeaux?

3. **cabernet**，读音：/ˌkæ.bɚ.ˈneɪ/，重音：主重音第3音节，中文：红葡萄酒；口语：① I'll have a glass of Cabernet, please. ② Does this Cabernet go well with steak?

4. **champagne**，读音：/ʃæm.ˈpeɪn/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：香槟酒；香槟酒色；口语：① Shall we open the champagne? ② I'd like a glass of champagne, please.

5. **sauvignon**，读音：/ˌsɔ.ˈvɪn.jɑn/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：索维农葡萄（产于法国的紫色甜葡萄）；口语：① Is this a Sauvignon Blanc? ② I'd like a glass of Sauvignon Blanc, please.

6. **sherry**，读音：/ˈʃɛ.ri/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：雪利酒；葡萄酒；口语：① Would you like a small glass of sherry? ② I use a little sherry in this sauce.

7. **vin**，读音：/vɪn/，重音：单音节，中文：（法）酒；葡萄酒；口语：① The French menu says vin rouge for red wine. ② Does vin blanc mean white wine?

8. **vineyard**，读音：/ˈvɪn.jɚd/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：葡萄园；口语：① The vineyard is just outside town. ② Can we walk through the vineyard?

9. **vineyards**，读音：/ˈvɪn.jɚdz/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：葡萄园( vineyard的复数形式 )；口语：① We drove past some beautiful vineyards. ② Which vineyards are open to visitors?

10. **vintage**，读音：/ˈvɪn.tɪdʒ/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：复古的；口语：① What vintage is this wine? ② That was a good vintage for this vineyard.

11. **winery**，读音：/ˈwaɪ.nɚ.i/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：葡萄酒酿造厂；酒庄；葡萄酒厂；口语：① We're visiting a winery this weekend. ② Does the winery offer tastings?

12. **wines**，读音：/waɪnz/，重音：单音节，中文：葡萄酒；令人沉醉的东西（wine的复数）；口语：① What wines do you serve by the glass? ② We tried three local wines.
