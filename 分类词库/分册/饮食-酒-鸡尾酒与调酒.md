# 饮食 / 酒 / 鸡尾酒与调酒

[返回分类目录](../README.md)

1. **cocktail**，读音：/ˈkɑk.ˌteɪl/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：鸡尾酒；开味品；口语：① Could you make me a cocktail without alcohol? ② What's your favorite cocktail?

2. **cocktails**，读音：/ˈkɑk.ˌteɪlz/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：鸡尾酒（cocktail的复数）；口语：① Let's get cocktails after work. ② Do you serve cocktails here?

3. **martini**，读音：/mɑr.ˈti.ni/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：马提尼（一种鸡尾酒；等于Martini）；口语：① I'll have a dry martini, please. ② Would you like an olive in your martini?

4. **sling**，读音：/slɪŋ/，重音：单音节，中文：投石器；抛掷；口语：① I'd like a Singapore sling, please. ② Have you ever tried a gin sling?
