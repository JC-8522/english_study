# 饮食 / 餐食 / 餐厅点餐与用餐

[返回分类目录](../README.md)

1. **all-you-can-eat**，读音：/ˌɔl ju kən ˈit/，重音：可重读 eat，中文：在规定范围内不限量供应的；口语：① Is that sushi place all-you-can-eat? ② The brunch is all-you-can-eat on weekends.

2. **bistro**，读音：/ˈbɪ.stroʊ/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：法式小餐馆；小酒馆；口语：① There's a nice little bistro around the corner. ② Shall we book a table at that French bistro?

3. **box up the leftovers**，读音：/bɑks ʌp ðə ˈlɛf.ˌtoʊ.vɚz/，重音：可重读 leftovers（主重音第1音节），中文：把没吃完的食物装盒带走；口语：① Could you box up the leftovers for us? ② I'll box up the leftovers when we're done.

4. **buffet**，读音：/ˈbʌ.fət/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：自助餐；小卖部；口语：① [Could you show me what you're serving in the buffet?](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/35106) ② [Where is the buffet restaurant?](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/13587132)

5. **cafes**，读音：/ˌkæ.ˈfeɪz/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：咖啡馆（cafe的复数）；口语：① [There are many cafes in Silvestre's city.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/10625651) ② [There are several cafes in the park.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/5603256)

6. **cafeteria**，读音：/ˌkæ.fə.ˈtɪ.ri.ə/，重音：主重音第3音节，中文：自助餐厅；口语：① [I don't eat at the school cafeteria anymore.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/5226722) ② [I'll buy you a coffee at the cafeteria.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/5832965)

7. **carte**，读音：/kɑrt/，重音：单音节，中文：(附有价目的)菜单；口语：① [You have carte blanche, you can do what you want.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/6581019) ② [You can dine here à la carte.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/8999600)

8. **diner**，读音：/ˈdaɪ.nɚ/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：用餐者；口语：① Can we talk about the diner for a minute? ② I still have a question about the diner.

9. **diners**，读音：/ˈdaɪ.nɚz/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：用餐者( diner的复数形式 )；口语：① [This patio can hold thirty diners.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/10164816) ② [A gust of wind knocked over a large umbrella onto the diners.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/6639406)

10. **make it to go**，读音：/meɪk ɪt tə ˈɡoʊ/，重音：可重读 go，中文：要求把食物打包带走；口语：① Could you make that to go? ② I ordered a sandwich and asked them to make it to go.

11. **menus**，读音：/ˈmɛn.juz/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：菜单（menu的复数形式）；口语：① [I don't often eat in restaurants with pictures of food on their menus.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/7544688) ② [Tom makes a lot of money translating menus for restaurants.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/1093220)

12. **mess**，读音：/mɛs/，重音：单音节，中文：食堂；伙食；口语：① [Do you realize the mess you're in here?](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/6382795) ② [We're the only people who can fix this mess.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/12093782)

13. **on the side**，读音：/ɑn ðə ˈsaɪd/，重音：可重读 side，中文：单独放在一旁，常用于点配菜或酱汁；口语：① Can I get the dressing on the side? ② I ordered the fries on the side.

14. **potluck**，读音：/ˈpɑt.ˌlʌk/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：每个人自带一道食物分享的聚餐；口语：① What are you bringing to the potluck? ② We're having a potluck at work on Friday.

15. **serve yourself**，读音：/sɝv jɚ.ˈsɛlf/，重音：可重读 yourself（主重音第2音节），中文：自己取用食物；口语：① Go ahead and serve yourself. ② There are plates by the stove if you want to serve yourself.

16. **set the table**，读音：/sɛt ðə ˈteɪ.bəl/，重音：可重读 table（主重音第1音节），中文：把餐具摆好准备吃饭；口语：① Could you set the table for four? ② I'll set the table while you finish cooking.

17. **split an appetizer**，读音：/splɪt ən ˈæ.pə.ˌtaɪ.zɚ/，重音：可重读 appetizer（主重音第1音节），中文：两人或多人分吃一份前菜；口语：① Do you want to split an appetizer? ② We split an appetizer while we waited.

18. **takeout**，读音：/ˈteɪ.ˌkaʊt/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：外带食品；口语：① Do you want to get takeout tonight? ② I picked up takeout on the way home.
