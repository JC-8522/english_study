# 饮食 / 饮料 / 咖啡与茶

[返回分类目录](../README.md)

1. **caffeine**，读音：/kæ.ˈfin/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：咖啡因；咖啡碱；口语：① [I've been trying to cut down on caffeine.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/1934742) ② [I'm getting a caffeine high from this excellent coffee.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/6601811)

2. **cha**，读音：/tʃɑ/，重音：单音节，中文：（英俚）茶；口语：① [I could do with a cup of cha.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/10181336) ② [He's playing a cha cha cha rhythm on the drum kit.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/4544613)

3. **chocolates**，读音：/ˈtʃɔ.kləts/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：巧克力糖（chocolate的复数形式）；口语：① [You like the slightly sour chocolates, don't you?](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/1312103) ② [How much does a box of chocolates cost?](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/9815210)

4. **cocoa**，读音：/ˈkoʊ.koʊ/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：可可粉；可可茶；口语：① [How about a cup of cocoa?](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/62158) ② [Do you like to put a marshmallow in your cocoa?](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/10618102)

5. **espresso**，读音：/ˌɛ.ˈsprɛ.soʊ/，重音：主重音第2音节，中文：（用汽加压煮出的）浓咖啡；口语：① [I'm loving this espresso machine.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/11350417) ② [Can I get a double espresso, please?](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/10593029)
