# 饮食 / 饮料 / 果汁软饮与其他饮品

[返回分类目录](../README.md)

1. **beverage**，读音：/ˈbɛ.vɚ.ɪdʒ/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：饮料；口语：① [Let's drink tea or some other beverage while waiting.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/2610363) ② [Tom's favourite beverage is Scotch whiskey.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/8091608)

2. **beverages**，读音：/ˈbɛv.rɪ.dʒɪz/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：饮料( beverage的复数形式 )；口语：① [Please don't bring alcoholic beverages into the stadium.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/4662821) ② [I don't like drinking beverages that are too sweet.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/13980375)

3. **coke**，读音：/koʊk/，重音：单音节，中文：可乐，Coke 是可口可乐的简称；小写 coke 口语还可指可卡因或焦炭；口语：① [I'm gonna stay here and finish my coke.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/8316972) ② [I haven't done coke in years.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/11483239)

4. **drinks**，读音：/drɪŋks/，重音：单音节，中文：饮料；饮品；口语：① [Why don't we order another round of drinks?](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/10070646) ② [Let's go out for a couple of drinks.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/6346211)

5. **juice**，读音：/dʒus/，重音：单音节，中文：汁；活力；口语：① [I'll buy the wine and the apple juice.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/3018162) ② [I don't like juice with pulp or seeds.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/3494966)

6. **juices**，读音：/ˈdʒu.səz/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：汁( juice的复数形式 )；果汁；口语：① [What kinds of fruit juices do you have?](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/10605208) ② [I should walk immediately on waking to get my juices flowing.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/6583425)
