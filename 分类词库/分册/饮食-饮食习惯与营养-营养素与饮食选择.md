5. **eater**，读音：/ˈi.tɚ/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：吃东西的人，常用搭配 a picky eater（挑食的人）；口语：① [I'm pretty sure Tom is a fussy eater.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/3171888) ② [I knew Tom wasn't a very fussy eater.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/7517405)

6. **edible**，读音：/ˈɛ.də.bəl/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：能吃的，可食用的；口语：① [What Tom made for lunch wasn't edible.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/8908288) ② [Can you tell edible and poisonous mushrooms apart?](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/12249288)

7. **fatty**，读音：/ˈfæ.ti/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：肥胖的；脂肪的；口语：① [I don't like fatty foods.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/2874436) ② [You shouldn't eat fatty foods.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/3824639)

9. **nutrients**，读音：/ˈnu.tri.ənts/，重音：主重音第1音节，中文：（食品或化学品）营养物；营养品( nutrient的复数形式 )；口语：① [Carbohydrates are one of the five essential nutrients.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/11148461) ② [This root vegetable is high on nutrients.](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/4311050)
